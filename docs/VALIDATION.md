# Validation notes

## What we have validated so far

Nothing with customers yet. Exitly is at idea / prototype stage. Figures in the pitch (tool counts, idle-seat cost, pricing) are working estimates.

## Automated pre-check (StartupDeckAI, free plan)

We ran the pitch deck through an AI validation tool. Treat it as a rough prompt for questions, not evidence.

- Verdict: **Pivot**, score **68 / 100**, confidence medium
- Strongest: market size (95), business model (95), timing (85)
- Weakest: defensibility (28), customer pain (50), founder edge (50)
- Its main concern: unproven demand for standalone offboarding software versus built-in HR/IT suites

**Caveat:** the tool reported that it could not extract text from the `.pptx`, so it worked from generic inputs. Its market sizing, its "gaps" (churn prediction, exit-interview analytics, customer health scoring) and its competitor list do not describe Exitly, and we do not rely on them. The defensibility and "no customers yet" concerns are fair and match our own stated risk.

## Questions we must answer

1. How will Exitly differentiate from the built-in offboarding in larger suites (e.g. Workday, Rippling) and from Zluri / Torii?
2. What evidence shows teams of 10 to 75 will pay $3 to $8 per person per month?
3. Which compliance framework (e.g. SOC 2) do early customers care about first?
