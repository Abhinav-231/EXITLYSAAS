# Roadmap

Start narrow, prove demand, then widen.

## Phase 0: Validate
- Interview 10 to 15 founders and operations leads
- Ask: "How do you handle offboarding today? What is the biggest headache with your workaround?"
- Recruit three pilot teams

## Phase 1: Prove it
- Google Workspace connection (Admin SDK, least-privilege scopes)
- Exit checklist
- Activity log

## Phase 2: Widen it
- Microsoft 365, GitHub, Slack
- HR-system link
- Ghost-account alerts (weekly scan)
- `/exit` command in Slack / Teams

## Phase 3: Sell it
- Spend discovery
- Audit-pack export
- Plan for IT consultants managing many small clients

## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Some apps lock user controls behind costly plans | Pair automation with guided checklists |
| We hold sensitive access | Minimal permissions, encryption, full activity log |
| Vendor interfaces change | Isolated, monitored connectors |
| Larger vendors add cheap plans | Win one narrow group first; compete on speed and buying experience |
